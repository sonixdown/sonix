This policy describes the information that may be handled when you visit the IPTV Sonic informational website. It does not cover independent third-party websites or services.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

About this website

IPTV Sonic is a static information site. It does not currently provide user accounts, checkout, streaming access, or a contact form. Links to a service operator or other third-party websites may be added or changed; IPTV Sonic does not control those sites' privacy practices.

Information that may be processed

When you load a website, the hosting provider and network infrastructure may process standard technical request data, such as IP address, requested page, date and time, browser and device details, and referring page. The hosting provider determines its own logs, security controls, and retention periods. No analytics or advertising tracking code is intentionally included on this site at the time of writing.

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and some photographs from Unsplash. Your browser may connect to those providers to retrieve these resources, and those providers may receive technical request information under their own policies.

Cookies and similar technologies

IPTV Sonic does not intentionally set account, advertising, or analytics cookies in the current static pages. The hosting provider or external websites and resources may use their own technical storage or cookies. Review the relevant provider policies and browser settings for more information.

External links

Links to independent service operators and other third parties open websites governed by their own terms and privacy notices. Information you provide there, including account, payment, or support details, is handled by that third party, not by IPTV Sonic.

Retention, security, and your choices

Technical log retention and security are managed by the website host. Because a direct privacy contact has not yet been configured, this page cannot receive access, correction, or deletion requests. Add a monitored privacy contact address before launch if you want visitors to send requests about this site.

Children and policy changes

This information site is not designed to collect information directly from children. This notice may be updated when site functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.

This policy is a practical description of the current static site, not a statement about any third-party service. Review it again if you add analytics, advertising, forms, accounts, or other data collection.
