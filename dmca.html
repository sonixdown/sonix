Where to send a copyright concern

For material published on the IPTV Sonic website itself, identify the exact page and material you believe infringes your rights. IPTV Sonic does not currently have a verified designated copyright-agent email or an online notice-submission form. A contact route must be configured before formal notices can be accepted through this website.

If your concern is about a channel, stream, playlist, account, or material on a separate streaming service, submit it directly through that service's official copyright-reporting channel. IPTV Sonic cannot remove material from an independent provider or decide a claim on its behalf.

Information a formal notice generally identifies

  • The copyrighted work and the specific material or URL being reported.
  • Your name and contact details, plus the rights owner you represent if you act as an agent.
  • A good-faith statement that the use is not authorized by the rights owner, its agent, or law.
  • A statement, made under applicable law, that the notice is accurate and you are authorized to act.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Counter-notices and repeat infringement

If content on a service has been removed after a notice, use that service's official counter-notice process. IPTV Sonic does not host user uploads or operate the third-party streaming service and cannot restore material removed by another provider.
