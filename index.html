What is IPTV Sonic?

IPTV Sonic is an informational landing page for live television and on-demand streaming offers. Subscription, trial, billing and account services are managed separately by the service provider.

What channels and on-demand content are listed?

The service provider advertises more than 55,000 live channels, 170,000 movies and 32,000 series. Catalog size, channel availability, and content rights can vary; confirm the current catalog and terms with the provider before subscribing.

Which streaming quality is available?

The service provider advertises Full HD, 4K and 8K streaming. Actual playback quality depends on the selected content, device, app, and connection.

What are the current Sonic IPTV prices?

The plan examples on this page may change. Check current pricing and plan details with the service provider before paying.

How do I start a trial or get help with an account?
